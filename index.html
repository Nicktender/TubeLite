About TubeLite

An independent, accountless interface. It uses YouTube's embedded player and public video search services; it does not load the YouTube website.
Search is provided by public Invidious instances and may occasionally be unavailable or rate-limited. Playback and live chat are provided by YouTube. Some videos cannot be embedded. Queue and recent history are kept only for this browser session.
